# Making Moles

Mini Chemistry game!

- learn how to convert moles (actual moles) to grams.
- learn how to prepare solutions using a flask

(coming soon) perform dilutions

## How to play
---
Converting moles to grams

1. Press the balance on the lab table.
2. Put in the amount of moles you want to be turned into grams. (max. 5)
3. Press record to get the solute.

---
Dissolving the solute

1. Press the flask on the lab table.
2. Choose the amount of volume you want.
3. Press the water beaker to add water up to half the flask.
4. Press the beaker with the solute to put it in the flask
5. Press the swirl button to swirl the flask around.
6. Press the water beaker to fill the flask up to the calibration line.
7. Press the shake button to shake the flask around.
---
